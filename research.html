Publications

  1. The Hidden Demand for AI Inside Your Company
    with Elena Alfaro, Antonio Cabrales, Luis Garicano, Toni Roldán Monés and Guillermo Vieira de Santiago
    Harvard Business Review, Apr 2026
    Abstract Read at HBR

    Most corporate GenAI programs disappoint. Since value is generated to a large extent from the bottom-up it is a good idea to use your employees’ technical and entrepreneurial talent. The job is to achieve this in a safe, visible, and scalable manner—fast—then build the organization around it. BBVA’s human-centered, bottom-up strategy demonstrates how to do that effectively at scale, in a heavily regulated industry.
