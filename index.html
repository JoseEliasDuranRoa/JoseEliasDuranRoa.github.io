Hello! 👋 I´m José Elías (Pepe), from Chile. I’m a PhD student in Economics at Universidad de Alicante, previously a researcher at Fedesarrollo. I'm currently in Paris, at the OECD Directorate of Education and Skills.

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Universidad de los Andes in Bogotá, Colombia, a MRes in Economic Analysis from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid and a Msc in Quantitative Economics from Universidad de Alicante.

My research focuses on Applied Econometrics, with a special interest in Economics of Education and how AI is reshaping labor dynamics within large firms.
